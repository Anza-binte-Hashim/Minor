Physiology

Physiology - Hero

Understanding the functions of living organisms and their systems is fundamental to medical science.

About Physiology

Physiology is the study of how living organisms and their parts work or function. It is the science of how organisms, organ systems, cells, and biomolecules carry out the chemical and physical functions that exist in a living system.

In medical practice, physiology helps doctors understand:

  • How the human body systems work
  • How organs function in health and disease
  • How cells respond to stimuli
  • How to diagnose and treat diseases

History of Physiology

The study of physiology dates back to ancient civilizations, but modern physiology began during the Renaissance.

Key milestones:

  • 1600s: William Harvey discovered blood circulation
  • 1800s: Claude Bernard established the concept of internal environment
  • 1900s: Development of modern experimental techniques

Anatomy

Anatomy - Hero

Anatomy is the science of understanding the structure of the human body and its components.

About Anatomy

Anatomy is the scientific study of the body's structures. It is an old science, having its beginnings in prehistoric times, with the first writings on anatomy dating back to the ancient Egyptians.

Anatomy is divided into several branches:

  • Gross Anatomy: Study of body structures visible to the naked eye
  • Microscopic Anatomy: Study of cells and tissues under microscope
  • Systemic Anatomy: Study of body systems
  • Regional Anatomy: Study of specific body regions

History of Anatomy

The study of anatomy has evolved significantly over the centuries.

Important developments:

  • Ancient Egypt: Mummification led to anatomical knowledge
  • Renaissance: Andreas Vesalius revolutionized anatomy with detailed observations
  • Modern Era: Advanced imaging techniques provide new insights
